Overview

What is this project?

ATOMI is a Brazilian brand that sells electric bikes and electric scooters. This landing page was created for a special wholesale promotion — the "Pack Showroom" Easter 2026 campaign — targeting resellers and distributors across Brazil.

The page replaces a traditional contact form with a direct WhatsApp sales flow, routing each potential buyer to the correct salesperson based on their Brazilian state. No friction, no waiting — one click to the right sales rep.

Core Features

What does it do?

  • Product showcase — 6 electric vehicles (bikes + scooters) with full specs, promo pricing, and discount badges
  • Geo-based seller routing — buyer selects their Brazilian state from a dropdown (all 27 states); each state maps to a dedicated sales rep
  • Seller modal — on state selection, a modal appears with the salesperson's photo, name, territory, and a direct WhatsApp button
  • No forms, no delays — every CTA leads directly to a personalized WhatsApp conversation with the right rep
  • 1-year warranty highlight — prominently displayed, compared to the industry standard of 6 months
  • Floating WhatsApp button — follows the user while scrolling; clicking it jumps to the state selector and highlights it
